# WannaCry Incident Timeline

## Overview

The WannaCry ransomware outbreak occurred in May 2017 and rapidly affected organizations and computer systems around the world.

## Timeline

### 12 May 2017 — Initial Global Outbreak

WannaCry began spreading rapidly across vulnerable Windows systems.

The malware exploited a vulnerability in the Server Message Block (SMBv1) protocol.

### 12 May 2017 — Rapid Propagation

After compromising vulnerable systems, WannaCry attempted to spread to additional systems across networks.

Organizations with unpatched Windows systems were particularly exposed.

### 12 May 2017 — Global Impact

The outbreak affected organizations across multiple countries and sectors, including healthcare, telecommunications, manufacturing and government organizations.

The UK's National Health Service was among the organizations significantly affected.

### 13 May 2017 — Kill Switch Domain Activation

A cybersecurity researcher discovered and registered a domain embedded within the malware.

The registration caused the original sample to stop spreading under certain conditions.

This did not eliminate the overall threat because modified variants could bypass the mechanism.

### Following Days — Security Response

Organizations began applying available Microsoft security updates, isolating infected systems and blocking vulnerable SMB traffic.

Security teams also investigated affected endpoints and implemented additional defensive controls.

## Key Lessons

The WannaCry outbreak demonstrated the importance of:

- Timely security patching
- Vulnerability management
- Network segmentation
- Endpoint monitoring
- Secure backups
- Incident response planning
